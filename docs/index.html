wavepacket · the Schrödinger equation, live

iħ ∂tψ = −(ħ²/2m)∇²ψ + Vψ, solved in your browser on a grid of up to 256² points with the split-step Fourier method. Drag on the picture to launch a wavepacket: where you press sets its position, and the drag sets its momentum.

t = 0

Colour = phase of ψ, brightness = |ψ|. The colour bands march at the de Broglie wavelength λ = 2π/k. Edges absorb outgoing waves, so ∫|ψ|² drops as the wave leaves.
